# 15 - WHERE Clause

## Objective

To learn how to filter records from the `Student` table using the `WHERE` clause and different SQL comparison, logical, range, membership, pattern-matching, and NULL operators.

## Task

Perform different filtering operations on the `Student` table.

The practical includes:

- Finding students whose age is greater than 20.
- Finding students from Kathmandu who are older than 18.
- Finding students who live in Kathmandu or Pokhara.
- Finding students whose age is between 18 and 22.
- Finding students whose `class_id` is 4, 5, or 6.
- Finding students whose names start with `R`.
- Finding students whose names end with `a`.
- Finding students whose address is `NULL`.

## SQL Concepts Learned

- `WHERE`
- Comparison Operator: `>`
- Logical Operators: `AND`, `OR`
- `BETWEEN`
- `IN`
- `LIKE`
- `IS NULL`
- Wildcards: `%`

The required student records were successfully filtered according to different conditions using the `WHERE` clause and SQL operators.
